## Download an audit archive

Use a platform-operator login and matching CLI/Purser versions:

```bash
frameworks admin billing finance audit --from inception --through now \
  --provider stripe --output ./ddvtech-financial-audit.zip
```

The command reads Purser records for tenants enumerated through Quartermaster
and the configured Stripe account. It performs no collections, refunds, payouts,
invoice corrections, or database writes. Both service calls and provider calls
are paginated. A failed page or a non-advancing cursor prevents publication of
the archive. An existing output file is never overwritten; the completed ZIP
has private filesystem permissions.

The ZIP includes:

- `manifest.json`: occurrence interval, tenant scope, source counts, page capture
  timestamps, file hashes and `posting_ready: false`.
- `evidence/*.jsonl`: retained invoice/payment/top-up/balance-movement records
  from Purser, plus Stripe account, current balance, invoices, charges, balance
  transactions and payouts. Imported provider documents include original document
  numbers, tax facts and PDF hashes; retained provider tax profiles include the
  customer identity and evidence used before collection.
- `exceptions.csv`: warnings grouped by tenant, source and action, with record counts.
- `exception_records.csv`: individual record IDs behind those warnings. Missing
  regular-invoice tax breakdowns remain visible; grouping does not waive them.

The manifest uses archive schema version 2. `--through now` is resolved by Purser
once and reused for every page; the laptop clock does not choose the cutoff.
Use matching CLI/Purser versions so both understand `effective_through`. Explicit
future cutoffs are rejected. This remains an observed evidence export: backdated
records and mutable statuses prevent treating it as a frozen accounting close.

`--from` accepts `inception`, a UTC date, or an RFC3339 timestamp; `--through`
accepts `now` or RFC3339 and is exclusive. Purser source rows contain their status
at capture time. Stripe account and balance snapshots are current at retrieval,
even when the list-activity interval is historical. This is not an accounting
snapshot reconstructed as of a past date.

Purser rows with no occurrence date are retained regardless of the requested
interval and flagged `OCCURRENCE_DATE_UNKNOWN`. Establish their accounting period
before using the archive for interval control totals.

`--provider none` omits provider evidence and records that omission explicitly.
Historical tenants absent from Quartermaster enumeration remain a coverage
exception; the archive does not certify that every retained tenant was included.
Stripe invoice records retain provider document references, but original PDF
files are not downloaded by this command.

Imported Stripe invoices and credit notes are available in the customer's
billing-document list. Downloads return the retained original PDF. Purser keeps
the provider's document number and does not issue another invoice for the same
Stripe subscription supply.

Stripe imports use the accepted supplier and customer snapshot and tax evidence
valid at the document's tax point. They do not depend on today's billing profile,
supplier configuration or a new VIES request. Original amounts, parties, document
numbers, PDF bytes and issued facts remain unchanged. Missing original tax
amounts are never invented to clear an exception.

An already-retained unverified document keeps that classification. Webhook
redelivery and the recurring tax worker do not reassess it. An operator can
resolve its review without the same facts reopening it or increasing its count.
Documents issued using provisional VAT evidence have their own retained review,
separate from the tenant's later VIES revalidation notice.
A postdeploy reconciliation inserts missing document reviews without requiring
webhook redelivery and preserves existing operator resolutions.

Jurisdiction policy references and validity windows form part of the accepted
provider assessment identity. Renewing that policy retains a new assessment for
future collections; older documents keep the proof valid at their own tax point.

Use this archive to investigate the opening records and prepare corrections with
the bookkeeper. It is not a journal import, reconciled close or payout approval.
The export intentionally supplies no `journal.csv` and never marks itself
posting-ready.

## Personal and business billing

Customers select **Personal** or **Business** in their saved billing details.
Personal profiles need no company name or VAT number. Choosing personal billing
clears those business fields; the backend rejects a consumer profile that still
claims a business VAT number. Existing profiles remain `unspecified` until
classified, rather than deriving tax status from their company name. A retained VAT claim
identifies a legacy business for assessment and establishment reviews; confirming
Business later preserves the same evidence identity. No saved selection is
changed by an operator evidence read.

For new crypto payment instructions, unclassified profiles without a VAT claim
must choose a customer type, and all profiles must confirm their billing country.
Cross-border tax decisions use a shared consumer/business rule engine. Consumer
location evidence does not establish business status or reverse charge.

The Dutch supplier policy adds 21% VAT to domestic prices and to EU consumer
prices. A qualifying EU business outside the Netherlands receives reverse
charge. Non-EU service documents state that Dutch VAT is outside scope. Prices
are exclusive of VAT. Customers do not choose a rate or exemption.

Purser freezes the net amount, VAT, gross amount, tax point, billing parties and
tax evidence when it issues an invoice. Invoice PDFs and emails show those
totals. Stripe subscriptions use exclusive tax rates selected from the same
assessment; Mollie subscription payments produce a Purser invoice from their
retained collection terms. Changes to a saved profile affect future collections,
not documents already issued.

New prepaid purchases collect the gross amount and add the net amount as
spendable service credit. A EUR 100 purchase with 21% VAT collects EUR 121 and
adds EUR 100 credit. The payment and its tax document commit together. Applying
that advance to a later invoice deducts its original net and VAT, so VAT is not
charged again. Refunds credit the original tax and remove only the refunded net
credit from the wallet.

Existing paid purchases and balances retain their accepted amounts. Where an
old balance has no retained tax breakdown, it remains unclassified; any VAT
covered by the supplier is stated separately when that credit is applied. The
upgrade does not initiate a second charge to recover historical VAT.

An explicitly configured destination policy supplies any additional destination
treatment. Business evidence retained for a configured reviewed jurisdiction is
bound to the saved identity. Profile changes invalidate that evidence.

## VIES availability and retained evidence

A quote retains the exact VIES response used for its decision. Confirmation
uses that accepted assessment without another VIES call. Legacy quotes use a
positive response that was valid when the quote was issued, when retained;
a verification outage must not silently replace reverse charge with VAT.
Without sufficient evidence, the received payment stays recorded and its
missing tax document remains in reconciliation review.

The latest positive VIES response remains usable for seven days after its
24-hour cache expires, only during a transient outage. A newer negative result
prevents fallback. Permanent SOAP faults are classified before the HTTP status,
so an HTTP 500 carrying a rejected VAT request does not authorize reverse charge.

Each use of dated evidence records durable, tenant-scoped revalidation work.
The billing worker rechecks it when VIES is available. A successful check retains
the later response beside the accepted claim; an invalid result remains an
operator exception. Issued facts and already-paid amounts remain unchanged.

## Tax holds and payment retries

Stripe collection holds require a definite tax-review condition. Temporary
database, configuration or VIES failures are retried without creating a new
collection hold. After billing details are corrected, Purser synchronizes future
tax rates and releases its own pause. Older invoices that still need review do
not prevent correctly taxed future renewals or other subscriptions from running.
Checkout and tier-upgrade preflights synchronize future tax settings; background
workers handle older held invoices. Completed recovery clears its pending marker,
so ordinary reconciliations do not keep listing historical invoices.
Incomplete recurring billing profiles remain visible as an operator review and
a completion prompt on the customer's Billing page. Completing the legal name,
email and address in Settings lets the worker synchronize future tax settings.

Draft renewals created during the tax hold receive the verified tax treatment
and return to Stripe's automatic collection. Stripe handles declined payments,
authentication requests and payments still processing through its normal
collection flow. Restoring tax settings does not require immediate payment
success. Unrelated invoices and operator-created pauses remain untouched;
finalized invoices retain their original tax facts.

Owned held drafts with prorations, discounts or a supply other than a regular
renewal receive a durable review for that invoice. Purser records the review
before acknowledging it to Stripe and completing the recovery scan. They are
not silently abandoned or retried by every tax sweep. Operators can correct the
invoice through Stripe and resolve its review. A deliberate `auto_advance=false`
on a finalized invoice is preserved.
Changing an operator pause or the subscription's customer rechecks outstanding
drafts against that new context. Finalized invoices stay outside draft repair.

For native Mollie payments, temporary assessment failures leave the webhook
available for retry before an invoice is issued. Definitive mismatches retain
the accepted payment terms and an operator review. Issuance checks the billing
identity and personal/business classification used by the assessment against
the saved profile before freezing the document.
Missing current supplier configuration uses the complete supplier evidence
already accepted with a confirmed payment, records a document review, and lets
the payment lifecycle proceed. Incomplete accepted evidence cannot be used to
invent an invoice supplier. Confirmed payments with unusable accepted tax proof
retain their actual received amount in a paid record requiring tax review,
without an assessed net/VAT split or an emailed tax invoice. A received amount
that differs from the accepted fee does not reset existing dunning attempts.
Grace-based VAT evidence has a document-specific
review that remains independent of the tenant's later revalidation notice.

Temporary VIES failures during postpaid issuance roll back that attempt and use
the normal job retry schedule, without a permanent manual-review invoice.

A tax refusal during a billing-phase change leaves the arrangement unchanged
and records an operator exception after rollback. Bootstrap dry runs do not
persist these exceptions.

## Reviewed non-EU tax policy

`CRYPTO_TAX_POLICY_JSON` accepts an explicit supplier country, review reference,
UTC validity window, and country-specific `non_eu_businesses` and/or
`non_eu_consumers` rules. Each rule requires `rate_bps` (including an explicit
zero), `source`, `evidence_reference`, `tax_label` and invoice `wording`. The
operator must obtain the actual jurisdiction treatment before configuring it.
For example, the following illustrates the format, not a tax determination:

```json
{
  "reference": "retained-tax-review",
  "supplier_country": "NL",
  "valid_from": "2026-11-01T00:00:00Z",
  "valid_until": "2027-01-01T00:00:00Z",
  "non_eu_businesses": {
    "US": {
      "rate_bps": 0,
      "source": "Reviewed treatment for this supply",
      "evidence_reference": "retained-country-memo",
      "tax_label": "VAT",
      "wording": "Outside scope of Dutch VAT"
    }
  }
}
```

A non-EU business also needs establishment evidence recorded by a platform
operator, bound to its complete saved business profile:

```bash
frameworks admin billing finance tax-profile --tenant-id <customer-uuid>
frameworks admin billing finance record-business-evidence \
  --tenant-id <customer-uuid> --profile-sha256 <returned-profile-sha256> \
  --expected-revision <returned-evidence-revision> \
  --reference <retained-establishment-proof> --valid-until <RFC3339-expiry>
```

Inspect the returned profile before approving it. An approval requires the exact
fingerprint and evidence revision inspected, expires within 366 days, and
retains the operator identity and reference for ten years. The command records
a reference; retain the underlying proof separately. Changing billing identity
invalidates the matching proof. Revoke all earlier approvals with:

```bash
frameworks admin billing finance record-business-evidence \
  --tenant-id <customer-uuid> --revoke --reference <revocation-reason>
```

Both inspection and recording require a platform-operator JWT; service tokens
and customer sessions cannot approve evidence. The audit archive includes the
append-only approval and revocation records. Neither a business review nor a
country policy enrolls the supplier in a foreign tax scheme.

Historical invoices without retained tax facts remain missing-tax exceptions.
New invoices with complete frozen totals and a supported policy do not receive
that warning. This archive remains evidence for bookkeeping: it does not produce
balanced journal entries, reconcile provider payouts to the bank, or certify an
accounting close.
