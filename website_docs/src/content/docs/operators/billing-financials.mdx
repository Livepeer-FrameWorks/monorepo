## Download an audit archive

Use a platform-operator login and matching CLI/Purser versions:

```bash
frameworks admin billing finance audit --from inception --through now \
  --provider stripe --output ./ddvtech-financial-audit.zip
```

The command reads Purser records for tenants enumerated through Quartermaster
and the configured Stripe account. It performs no collections, refunds, payouts,
invoice corrections, or database writes. Both service calls and provider calls
are paginated. A failed page or a non-advancing cursor prevents publication of
the archive. An existing output file is never overwritten; the completed ZIP
has private filesystem permissions.

The ZIP includes:

- `manifest.json`: occurrence interval, tenant scope, source counts, page capture
  timestamps, file hashes and `posting_ready: false`.
- `evidence/*.jsonl`: retained invoice/payment/top-up/balance-movement records
  from Purser, plus Stripe account, current balance, invoices, charges, balance
  transactions and payouts.
- `exceptions.csv`: missing regular-invoice tax breakdowns and source/evidence
  limitations requiring review.

`--from` accepts `inception`, a UTC date, or an RFC3339 timestamp; `--through`
accepts `now` or RFC3339 and is exclusive. Purser source rows contain their status
at capture time. Stripe account and balance snapshots are current at retrieval,
even when the list-activity interval is historical. This is not an accounting
snapshot reconstructed as of a past date.

Purser rows with no occurrence date are retained regardless of the requested
interval and flagged `OCCURRENCE_DATE_UNKNOWN`. Establish their accounting period
before using the archive for interval control totals.

`--provider none` omits provider evidence and records that omission explicitly.
Historical tenants absent from Quartermaster enumeration remain a coverage
exception; the archive does not certify that every retained tenant was included.
Stripe invoice records retain provider document references, but original PDF
files are not downloaded by this command.

Use this archive to investigate the opening records and prepare corrections with
the bookkeeper. It is not a journal import, reconciled close or payout approval.
The export intentionally supplies no `journal.csv` and never marks itself
posting-ready.

## Personal and business billing

Customers select **Personal** or **Business** in their saved billing details.
Personal profiles need no company name or VAT number. Choosing personal billing
clears those business fields; the backend rejects a consumer profile that still
claims a business VAT number. Existing profiles remain `unspecified` until
classified, rather than deriving tax status from their company name.

For new crypto payment instructions, unclassified profiles without a VAT claim
must choose a customer type, and all profiles must confirm their billing country.
Cross-border tax decisions use a shared consumer/business rule engine. Consumer
location evidence does not establish business status or reverse charge.

`EU_CONSUMER_VAT_MODE=destination` selects destination-country consumer VAT.
`EU_CONSUMER_DESTINATION_REGISTERED=true` records the operator's confirmation
that OSS or applicable destination registration is in place. These settings do
not register the supplier or file returns. Leave them unconfirmed until the
seller's actual fiscal position is established.

The rule engine also supports the conditional `origin_threshold` regime, including
the purchase that crosses the threshold and the previous calendar year. The
current crypto adapter has no verified annual turnover feed and holds that
regime for review. Non-EU consumer and business destinations require a reviewed
jurisdiction policy; country alone never produces an automatic exemption.

Regular postpaid tax finalization, Stripe recurring invoice normalization,
prepaid net-credit migration, and posting-ready accounting exports require the
remaining billing implementation. This evidence command and consumer safeguards
do not certify those paths as tax-complete.
