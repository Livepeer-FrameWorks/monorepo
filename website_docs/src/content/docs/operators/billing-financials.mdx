Purser issues documents for the configured seller. DDVTech B.V.'s hosted-service
documents are DDVTech's sales records. Stripe and Mollie collect payments; their
payouts transfer money from provider balances to the bank.

## Download the evidence

Use a platform-operator login and matching CLI/Purser versions:

```bash
frameworks admin billing finance audit --from inception --through now \
  --provider stripe --output ./ddvtech-financial-audit.zip
```

This reads Purser records for tenants enumerated through Quartermaster and the
configured Stripe account. It performs no collections, refunds, payouts, invoice
corrections or database writes. Service and provider reads are paginated. A failed
page or non-advancing cursor prevents publication; existing output files are not
overwritten. The completed ZIP has private filesystem permissions.

The archive contains:

- `manifest.json`: interval, tenant scope, source counts, capture timestamps,
  hashes and `posting_ready: false`.
- `evidence/*.jsonl`: Purser documents, payments, balances, reviews and accepted
  provider tax profiles; optional Stripe account, balance, invoice, charge,
  balance-transaction and payout evidence.
- `exceptions.csv`: grouped warnings and required actions.
- `exception_records.csv`: individual source identities behind those warnings.

`--from` accepts `inception`, a UTC date or RFC3339 timestamp. `--through` accepts
`now` or RFC3339 and is exclusive. Purser resolves `now` once for every page;
future cutoffs are rejected. Undated rows remain included and flagged for period
review. Use `--provider none` to omit provider evidence explicitly.

Archive schema version is 2. Statuses are observed at capture; backdated records
and later changes prevent it being a frozen close. Stripe account/balance
snapshots are current at retrieval, including for historical intervals. Tenants
absent from Quartermaster enumeration remain a coverage exception. Original PDF
references/hashes are included, but this command does not download PDF files.
Customers download retained originals through their billing-document list.

## Inspect and acknowledge tax reviews

List a tenant's open reviews:

```bash
frameworks admin billing finance reviews list --tenant-id <customer-uuid>
```

The list includes retained evidence and its hash. Optional `--provider`,
`--category` and `--status open|resolved|all` filters narrow it. After inspecting
the actual evidence, record a decision:

```bash
frameworks admin billing finance reviews acknowledge \
  --tenant-id <customer-uuid> --review-id <review-uuid> \
  --evidence-sha256 <inspected-hash> --reason "<review decision>"
```

Acknowledgment retains the operator identity and reason. It does not change an
invoice, validate a VAT number, move money or dismiss funds/scanner failures.
The same evidence does not reopen a resolved review; new facts can. An older
review's acknowledgment does not resolve a later invoice's separate review.

## Customer tax and invoices

Prices exclude VAT. With the Dutch supplier service policy, Dutch customers and
EU consumers pay Dutch VAT, currently 21%. A qualifying business elsewhere in
the EU receives reverse charge after validation. Non-EU services state that
Dutch VAT is outside scope. Customers save their location and choose Personal
or Business; they do not select a rate or exemption.

A subscription that existed before tax assessment shipped keeps its earlier
treatment until the customer saves a complete billing profile, starts a new
checkout or upgrades the plan. Until then Purser does not change its Stripe or
Mollie customer, tax IDs, subscription tax rates or collection amount, and its
recurring Purser invoices are issued for the net amount without a VAT line or a
tax review hold. Those invoices remain marked `legacy_unknown` in the finance
export. The `tenant_subscriptions.tax_assessed_since` timestamp records when a
customer moved to assessed handling.

New purchases require relevant billing details and claim validation. Once a
subscription uses assessed handling, recurring Stripe/Mollie collections use
Dutch VAT while details are incomplete or a new/changed EU VAT claim cannot be
established. The Billing page shows a notice, and each affected invoice gets its
own operator review. Temporary database failures leave provider settings
unchanged for retry. Corrected details affect future collections.

Purser invoices freeze parties, tax point, net/VAT/gross and accepted evidence.
Stripe subscription supplies retain Stripe's original number and PDF without a
duplicate Purser supply. Mollie payments use accepted collection terms for a
Purser invoice. Confirmed payments with insufficient legal evidence remain
recorded for review without an invented tax invoice.

Prepaid purchases collect gross cash and grant net service credit. EUR 121 at
21% VAT grants EUR 100 credit. Consuming it carries its original tax and does not
charge VAT again. Refunds retain original tax treatment. Previously paid
customers are not charged again to recover historical VAT.

## VIES and original evidence

VIES proof is retained with accepted assessments. During a transient outage, a
positive result can remain usable for seven days after its 24-hour cache expires.
A newer negative response or permanent fault prevents this. Provisional proof
creates revalidation work and a separate document review. An unchanged,
previously established reverse-charge setup keeps its actual provider terms
during a transient outage and retries verification. New or changed unproven
claims use Dutch VAT and review. Reconciliation compares the complete legal
profile and retained setup, rather than assuming that any old VAT number proves
an unchanged claim.

Issued documents are checked against evidence valid at their tax point. Today's
profile, supplier configuration and VIES response do not rewrite their money,
parties, number or PDF. Redelivery does not automatically promote an already
retained unknown document to verified. Keep corrections linked to their original
issuer and supporting evidence.

## Hand off to bookkeeping

Give the archive and original invoices/credit notes to the person preparing the
accounts. Match payments against provider activity, then reconcile fees, refunds,
disputes, reserves, currency conversion and payouts to bank receipts. Provider
fee invoices are separate purchase evidence. A payout is not another sale;
receipts and prepaid statements must not duplicate invoice revenue.

Resolve missing-tax, missing-document and coverage exceptions explicitly. The
archive supplies no `journal.csv`, balanced journal, reconciled prepaid schedule,
bank match or close approval. Moving money from Stripe does not remove the need
to account for the associated sales, tax and fees.
