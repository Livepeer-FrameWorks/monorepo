## SessionQoeSummary

**object**

Viewer-experienced QoE summary. Ratios are sum(numerator)/sum(denominator) over the player-reported session beacons; rebufferingRatio is the headline QoE number.

- **sessionCount**: `Int!`
- **playedHours**: `Float!`
- **rebufferingRatio**: `Float!`
- **rebuffersPerHour**: `Float!`
- **avgRebufferMs**: `Float!`
- **frameDropRatio**: `Float!`
- **playbackFailureRate**: `Float!`
- **ebvsRate**: `Float!`
- **avgBitrateBps**: `Float!`
- **abrSwitchesPerHour**: `Float!`
- **avgLiveEdgeLatencyMs**: `Float!`

## SessionQoeTimeSeriesBucket

**object**

One toStartOfInterval window of the viewer-experienced QoE summary. Ratios are sum(numerator)/sum(denominator) over the per-session rollup; `sessionCount` and `playedHours` are the per-bucket denominators.

- **timestamp**: `Time!` ([Time](/builders/api-schema/shared/#time))
- **sessionCount**: `Int!`
- **playedHours**: `Float!`
- **rebufferingRatio**: `Float!`
- **frameDropRatio**: `Float!`
- **avgBitrateBps**: `Float!`

## SortOrder

**enum**

Sort direction for ordered results.

- `ASC` — Ascending order (oldest first, A-Z).
- `DESC` — Descending order (newest first, Z-A).

## StreamSummarySortField

**enum**

Sort field for stream analytics summaries.

- `EGRESS_GB` — Sort by egress bandwidth (cost attribution).
- `UNIQUE_VIEWERS` — Sort by unique viewers (engagement).
- `TOTAL_VIEWS` — Sort by total views (popularity).
- `VIEWER_HOURS` — Sort by viewer hours (watch time).

## VodRetention

**object**

VOD retention curve for an artifact. retention(T) = points[T].reached / totalSessions (monotonic non-increasing); density(T) = points[T].secondsWatched (the &#34;most replayed&#34; curve). Buckets are fixed-width (bucketWidthS) along the asset timeline. These differ because a seek-to-end raises reach without adding density.

- **bucketWidthS**: `Int!`
- **assetDurationS**: `Int!`
- **totalSessions**: `Int!`
- **points**: `[VodRetentionPoint!]!` ([VodRetentionPoint](/builders/api-schema/analytics-types-s-v/#vodretentionpoint))

## VodRetentionAsset

**object**

A VOD asset with retention data in the window. Eligibility + stats (sessions, duration, lastSeen) come from analytics; title/playbackId are composed from the catalog by artifactHash — both may be null when the asset is uncatalogued (e.g. deleted but retention still within TTL).

- **artifactHash**: `String!`
- **totalSessions**: `Int!`
- **durationS**: `Int!`
- **lastSeen**: `Time!` ([Time](/builders/api-schema/shared/#time))
- **title**: `String`
- **playbackId**: `String`

## VodRetentionPoint

**object**

One point of the VOD retention curve: a fixed-width timeline bucket with its watched-seconds density and the count of sessions that reached it.

- **bucketIndex**: `Int!`
- **secondsWatched**: `Float!`
- **reached**: `Int!` — Sessions whose furthest playhead position is at or beyond this bucket (audience retention numerator).
