StreamCrafter is the live studio in your dashboard. It captures your camera and screen in a browser tab, mixes them, and sends the result to FrameWorks. You don't need OBS or any other software.

For a long or high-stakes broadcast, a dedicated encoder is still the steadier choice: see [Encoder Setup](/builders/encoder-setup).

## Before you start

- **A stream to broadcast to.** Open **Content → Streams** and create one if you don't have one yet. Pick a stream that receives pushed video; streams that pull from a camera or another server don't appear in Go Live.
- **A Chromium-based browser, such as Chrome or Edge, is the safest choice.** In other browsers, your broadcast may pause when you switch away from the tab.
- **Upload speed to match your quality setting.** The default needs about 4.5 Mbps, and more is better.

## 1. Pick a stream

Open **Go Live** in the dashboard sidebar and choose your stream under **Broadcast to**.

## 2. Prepare the connection

Click **Prepare connection**. FrameWorks finds a recommended node for your stream and shows its region, and the studio appears underneath.

If no destination is confirmed, check your stream access and try again. FrameWorks looks up the best node again each time you connect, so the one shown here can change.

## 3. Add your sources

Use the buttons along the bottom of the studio:

- **Add Camera** starts your camera and microphone. Allow access when the browser asks.
- **Share Screen** lets you choose a screen, a window, or a browser tab.

Each source appears in the mixer, where you can mute it, hide it from the picture, or remove it. You can add and remove sources during a broadcast.

<Aside type="caution" title="Sharing your entire screen">
  The studio's right-click menu can open an advanced panel that shows your private connection
  address. Keep that panel closed, or share a single window or tab instead of your entire screen.
</Aside>

## 4. Choose a layout

With two or more sources, the layout controls on the preview arrange them. You can show one source alone, place one inside another as picture-in-picture, or use a split, focus, or grid layout. The cycle control swaps which source goes in which position.

Your browser does the mixing, so more sources put more load on your computer.

## 5. Rehearse

Nothing is public until you click **Go Live**, so use the preview to check everything first:

- Check your framing and layout.
- Watch the audio meter while you speak.
- Open the settings button to choose a quality setting. You can't change it during a broadcast.

| Setting      | Video           | Use it for                        |
| ------------ | --------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Professional | 1080p, 8 Mbps   | A fast, stable upload             |
| Broadcast    | 1080p, 4.5 Mbps | Most streams. This is the default |
| Conference   | 720p, 2.5 Mbps  | Limited upload, meetings          |

## 6. Go live

Click **Go Live**. The button stays disabled until you've added a source. A **LIVE** indicator appears at the top of the page when your broadcast starts.

Keep the tab open for the whole broadcast. To check what viewers see, open the stream in **Content → Streams** in a second window and go to the **Playback** tab, which also has the playback link and embed code.

## 7. Send it to other platforms

To stream to YouTube, Twitch, or anywhere else at the same time, open your stream in **Content → Streams** and add destinations on its **Multistream** tab. Enabled destinations start and stop with your broadcast. See [Multistreaming](/builders/multistreaming).

## If something goes wrong

- **The connection drops.** The studio shows **Reconnecting...** and retries a few times on its own. If it shows an error instead, reload the page and repeat these steps. You'll need to add your sources again.
- **The browser blocks your camera or microphone.** Allow access in the browser's site settings, then reload the page.
- **The picture freezes when you change tabs.** Your browser is pausing the encoder in the background. Stay on the tab, or switch to a Chromium-based browser.
- **Video is choppy.** Close other applications, remove sources you don't need, or switch to the Conference setting before your next broadcast.
- **The stream won't start on a prepaid account.** At €0 new streams are blocked, and a broadcast that disconnects can't reconnect until you [top up](/builders/billing).

More fixes are in [Troubleshooting](/builders/troubleshooting).

## Finish the broadcast

Click **Stop Streaming**. Your multistream destinations stop with it.
