StreamCrafter is the live studio in your dashboard. It captures your cameras and screen in a browser tab, mixes them there, and sends the result to FrameWorks. You don't need OBS or any other software.

For a long or high-stakes broadcast, a dedicated encoder is still the steadier choice: see [Encoder Setup](/builders/encoder-setup).

## Before you start

- **A stream to broadcast to.** Open **Content → Streams** and create one if you don't have one yet. Pick a stream that receives pushed video; streams that pull from a camera or another server don't appear in Go Live.
- **A Chromium-based browser, such as Chrome or Edge, is the safest choice.** In other browsers, your broadcast may pause when you switch away from the tab.
- **Upload speed to match your quality setting.** The default needs about 4.5 Mbps, and more is better.

## 1. Pick a stream

Open **Go Live** in the dashboard sidebar and choose your stream under **Broadcast to**. A link from a stream page opens Go Live with that stream already chosen.

FrameWorks then finds an ingest node for the stream and opens the studio underneath. The line under the stream picker shows whether recording is on, and the node's address and region once it is found. FrameWorks looks up the best node again each time you go live, so the node can change between broadcasts.

If no node is found, a message under the picker says why and offers the next step, such as **Try again**, **Open stream keys**, or **Open billing**.

## 2. Add your sources

The action bar at the bottom of the studio has a button for each kind of source. Hover a button to see its name.

- **Add Camera** starts your camera and microphone. Allow access when the browser asks. If your computer has more than one camera, the button lists them, and you can add another camera at any time. A camera already in use is marked as active. Only the first camera brings a microphone.
- **Share Screen** lets you choose a screen, a window, or a browser tab.
- **Add media file** plays a video or audio file from your computer through the mix like any other source. The file plays from your browser tab and is not uploaded, so keep the tab open while it is in the mix. **Play**, **Pause**, and **Loop** for each file are in the **Media** section of the panel under the preview.

Each source appears in the mixer, where you can mute it, hide it from the picture, or remove it. You can add sources during a broadcast, but you can't remove one until the broadcast ends.

Once a source is capturing, the **Audio** section under the preview has the browser's own processing (echo cancellation, noise suppression, automatic gain) and the studio's noise gate, de-esser, EQ, and a setting that lowers other sources while the camera microphone picks up sound.

## 3. Choose a layout

With two or more sources, the layout controls on the preview arrange them. You can show one source alone, place one inside another as picture-in-picture, or use a split, focus, or grid layout. The cycle control swaps which source goes in which position.

The mix runs in your browser, so every source you add puts more load on your computer. A new source starts full-frame on top until you pick a layout.

### Scenes and transitions

A scene is a saved arrangement of sources. The panel under the preview lists your scenes and the layers of the scene you're on.

- **Add a scene** with the **+** button. A new scene starts empty: while you're on it, click the eye next to a source in the mixer to put that source in the scene.
- **Switch scenes** by clicking one, or with the **]** and **[** keys while the studio has focus. The transition menu next to the scene list sets how the picture changes: a cut, a fade, or a slide, with a duration in milliseconds.
- **Stack layers** with the arrows. The top of the list is drawn on top. The eye hides a layer in this scene and keeps its place in the stack.

Rehearse your transitions in the preview before you go live.

### Logos and text

The **Overlays** section of the panel puts a logo or text above your sources in the scene you're on. Overlays stay on top and keep their position when you change the layout.

- **Add logo** takes an image file, such as a PNG with a transparent background or an SVG. Set its size and position with the sliders. The image file stays in your browser; only the finished mix is sent.
- **Add text** puts a lower third near the bottom left. Type your text, then set its size, color, and position. The picture updates as you edit.

### Saved layouts

The studio saves the scenes, overlays, and layout of each stream in this browser. When you open a stream again, or pick another stream under **Broadcast to**, that stream's saved layout comes back; a stream without one starts from the default layout. The sources you added stay in the studio when you switch streams and join the layout of the stream you switched to. **Broadcast to** and the studio settings are locked while you are live, so end the broadcast before you switch.

Sources themselves are not saved: after a reload, add your camera, screen, and media files again. A saved layout stays in this browser only. Another browser or computer starts from the default layout, and clearing this site's data removes it.

### Studio settings

The gear next to **Broadcast to** opens **Studio settings**. **Compositor** turns scenes, layers, and layouts on or off, and **Renderer** picks how the browser draws the mix. Leave both at their defaults unless the studio reports that the compositor could not start. Changes apply right away, and your sources, scenes, and overlays stay in place. Turning the compositor off and on again brings back the scenes and overlays you had.

## 4. Rehearse

Nothing is public until you click **Go Live**, so use the preview to check everything first:

- Check your framing and layout.
- Watch the audio meter while you speak.
- Open the settings button in the action bar to choose a quality setting. You can't change it during a broadcast.

| Setting      | Video           | Use it for                        |
| ------------ | --------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Professional | 1080p, 8 Mbps   | A fast, stable upload             |
| Broadcast    | 1080p, 4.5 Mbps | Most streams. This is the default |
| Conference   | 720p, 2.5 Mbps  | Limited upload, meetings          |

The **Encoder** section under the quality settings overrides single values of the chosen setting:
video codec, resolution, framerate, video bitrate, and audio bitrate. **Reset to Profile** returns
to the setting's own values. The encoder is also locked during a broadcast.

## 5. Go live

Click **Go Live** at the right end of the action bar. The button stays disabled until you've added a source. A **LIVE** indicator appears at the top of the page when your broadcast starts, and the connection indicator in the studio header shows whether the upload is good, unstable, or poor; hover it for the bitrate, packet loss, and round trip.

Keep the tab open for the whole broadcast. To check what viewers see, open the stream in **Content → Streams** in a second window and go to the **Playback** tab, which also has the playback link and embed code.

## 6. Send it to other platforms

To stream to YouTube, Twitch, or anywhere else at the same time, open your stream in **Content → Streams** and add destinations on its **Multistream** tab. Enabled destinations start and stop with your broadcast. See [Multistreaming](/builders/multistreaming).

## If something goes wrong

- **The connection drops.** The studio shows **Reconnecting...** and retries a few times on its own. If it shows an error instead, click **Go Live** again. Your sources stay in place.
- **The browser blocks your camera or microphone.** Allow camera and microphone access for this site in the browser's site settings, then add the source again. Your other sources and scenes stay in place. Don't reload the page: a reload stops your sources, and you would add them again. Your scenes and overlays come back from the stream's saved layout.
- **The picture freezes when you change tabs.** Your browser is pausing the encoder in the background. Stay on the tab, or switch to a Chromium-based browser.
- **Video is choppy.** Close other applications, remove sources you don't need, or switch to the Conference setting before your next broadcast.
- **The stream won't start on a prepaid account.** At €0 new broadcasts are refused, and a broadcast that disconnects can't reconnect until you [top up](/builders/billing). A broadcast that is already running keeps going, and stops if the balance falls below -€10.

More fixes are in [Troubleshooting](/builders/troubleshooting).

## Finish the broadcast

Click **Stop Streaming**. Your multistream destinations stop with it.
